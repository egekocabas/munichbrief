Contact controls

Settings are saved immediately and survive restarts. An enquiry or email already being processed may complete before a change takes effect.

{{if not .ContactConfigured}}

Contact controls require protected administration, a database and MUNICHBRIEF_CONTACT_SECRET.

{{end}}

Accept form submissions

When closed, visitors can email contact@munichbrief.de. Existing enquiries and their queued notifications remain available.

Send email notifications

When off, new enquiries stay in the inbox only. They will not be emailed automatically later. Previously queued notifications resume when enabled.

{{if not .NotificationsConfigured}}

Sending is paused because email configuration is unavailable. The switch shows the saved setting; you can turn it off before configuring MUNICHBRIEF_SMTP2GO_API_KEY.

{{end}}
